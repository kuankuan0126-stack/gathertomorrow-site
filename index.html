How it works in practice

Case study

Payment-reminder automation

  1. Invoices live in a Google Sheet: customer, amount, due date, status.
  2. A Make scenario checks the sheet on a schedule for overdue, unpaid rows.
  3. For each one, it creates a personalized Gmail draft — nothing is sent automatically.
  4. The owner reviews, edits if needed, and sends. The sheet records that a reminder went out.
