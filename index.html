Examples

Payment-reminder automation

One workflow for overdue invoices. Nothing is sent automatically.

  1. 01

    Before · human

    How it was done

    Chasing unpaid invoices means checking a spreadsheet, writing the same polite email again, and remembering who you already nudged.

    Before: manual checking and copy-paste.

  2. 02

    What gets built

    The workflow

    1. Invoices live in a Google Sheet: customer, amount, due date, status.
    2. Automatic A Make scenario checks the sheet on a schedule for overdue, unpaid rows.
    3. Automatic For each one, it creates a personalized Gmail draft — nothing is sent automatically.
  3. 03

    Waiting for your review

    How you operate it

    The owner reviews, edits if needed, and sends. The sheet records that a reminder went out.

    After: drafts waiting in your inbox, you stay in control.
